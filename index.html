4D. Incorporating Risk & Protective Effects in Rare-Variant Meta-Regression: Model 2 (M8: 4-d.f. Joint Location + Slope) & Model 3 (M9: 3-d.f. Folded-Normal Magnitude Dispersion & Bidirectional Omnibus)

Standard 3-d.f. functional slope meta-regression (M7) tests only whether variant log-odds ratios covary monotonically with functional severity (H0: γAM = γLoF = γCon = 0) after centering out the gene-level intercept β0g. Consequently, M7 can miss two critical classes of disease genes: (1) Uniform Net Protective or Risk Coding Shifts (e.g., ACVR2A, SENP8, DNMT3A, TNFRSF6B), where damaging coding variants shift the entire gene baseline relative to neutral synonymous variants (Δβ0g ≠ 0, Zloc,g ≠ 0); and (2) Within-Gene Bidirectional Allelic Series (e.g., CSF2RB, CTSA, ZNF341, LPIN1, HECTD1), where some damaging variants confer risk (+βgv > 0) while others confer protection (−βgv < 0), causing signed slopes to cancel out.

Model 2 (M8): Joint 4-d.f. Location + Slope Omnibus Meta-Regression

Calibrates each gene's non-synonymous inverse-variance mean against the cohort × Firth-fallback synonymous neutral baseline (μ̂0,syn) to form an orthogonal 1-d.f. net coding location shift Zloc,g (< 0 for net protective coding burden; > 0 for net risk coding burden), unified with the 3-d.f. within-gene moderator slope test F3,Mg−4M7:

F4, Mg−4M8 = ( Zloc,g² + 3 · F3, Mg−4M7 ) / 4 ∼ F(4, Mg − 4)
Newly Rescued Loci in IBD (pM8 < 3.4×10−7, Missed by M7 Alone):
• Net Risk Shift (Zloc > 0): TNFRSF6B (Decoy Receptor 3, CD Zloc = +6.87, pM7 = 0.0105 → pM8 = 1.37×10−10; IBD Zloc = +7.09, pM8 = 2.37×10−10), SBNO2 (IBD Zloc = +4.41, pM8 = 2.64×10−10), CSF2RB (IBD Zloc = +3.12, pM8 = 6.93×10−8), RPS6KA1 (CD Zloc = +2.96, pM8 = 2.40×10−7).
• Net Protective Shift (Zloc < 0): ACVR2A (Activin Receptor IIA, UC Zloc = −6.11, pM7 = 2.70×10−3 → pM8 = 1.09×10−8), SMURF1 (CD Zloc = −4.50, pM8 = 6.53×10−9), VEPH1 (IBD Zloc = −5.60, pM8 = 1.01×10−8), SMARCA2 (CD Zloc = −4.23, pM8 = 1.98×10−8), NDUFB10 (UC/CD pM8 = 2.76×10−8), RUNX1 (UC Zloc = −4.10, pM8 = 5.33×10−8), SCNN1B (UC Zloc = −3.07, pM8 = 8.04×10−8), SENP8 (CD Zloc = −5.57, pM8 = 1.24×10−7), DNMT3A (CD Zloc = −5.81, pM8 = 2.31×10−7).

Model 3 (M9): 3-d.f. Folded-Normal Magnitude Dispersion & Bidirectional Omnibus

Instead of estimating noisy latent 2-component EM mixture proportions (πg), Model 3 (M9) directly regresses centered absolute variant effect magnitudes ugv = |β̂gv − β̄w,g| onto the 3 orthogonal functional moderators (AlphaMissense, pLoF, WES HMM constraint) via 3-d.f. Folded-Normal Weighted Least Squares (Fmag,g, pmag,g), unified with pM7,g via the equal-weight Cauchy combination test:

E[ |β̂gv − β̄w,g| | Xgv ] = α0g + αAM,g XAM + αLoF,g XLoF + αCon,g XCon  ⇒  pM9,g = ACAT(pM7,g, pmag,g)

ⓘ Why Are Canonical Low-Frequency/Common Protective IBD Loci (CARD9 & IL23R) Not Significant in the AF ≤ 0.001 Rare-Variant Scan?
In classical GWAS and unstratified sequencing studies, the best-known protective IBD alleles are the CARD9 C-terminal splice-site variant (c.1434+1G>C / rs141992399, minor allele frequency AF ≈ 0.0045 = 0.45%) and the IL23R coding variant (p.R381Q / rs11209026, AF ≈ 0.060 = 6.0%). Because both canonical protective alleles have minor allele frequencies well above the strict AF ≤ 0.001 (0.1%) and AF ≤ 0.0001 (0.01%) rare-variant thresholds, they are excluded from the rare-variant set. Across the remaining Mg = 307 rare non-synonymous variants (AF ≤ 0.001) within CARD9 in Crohn's disease, CARD9 is not significant in any rare-variant model: pM7 = 0.384 (CD), 0.759 (UC), 0.559 (IBD); pM8 = 0.518 (CD, net rare coding location shift Zloc = −0.36); and pM9 = 0.171 (CD, pmag = 0.103). Likewise, rare coding variants in IL23R (AF ≤ 0.001) show a single-component risk slope (pM7 = 1.63×10−5, pM9 = 4.62×10−5) below the exome-wide threshold (p < 3.4×10−7). Instead, at AF ≤ 0.001, Model 2 (M8) and Model 3 (M9) discover genuinely exome-wide significant rare protective shifts in ACVR2A (Zloc = −6.11, pM8 = 1.09×10−8), SMURF1 (Zloc = −4.50, pM8 = 6.53×10−9), SENP8 (Zloc = −5.57, pM8 = 1.24×10−7), and DNMT3A (Zloc = −5.81, pM8 = 2.31×10−7), and magnitude-dispersion allelic series in CTSA (pM9 = 1.76×10−12), CSF2RB (pM9 = 2.80×10−10), ZNF341 (pM9 = 3.00×10−10), HECTD1 (pM9 = 3.27×10−9), and LPIN1 (pM9 = 3.38×10−9).
⚠ Statistical Diagnostic: Why 2-Component EM Mixture Proportions (πg) Have Excessive Uncertainty & Low Power in Rare-Variant Summary Meta-Regression (95% Confidence Intervals for πg)

A natural question is whether one can estimate a gene-level 2-component Expectation–Maximization (EM) mixture proportion πg ∈ (0, 1) (representing the prior fraction of risk vs. protective alleles) alongside separate component slope vectors (γg(+), γg(−)). Rigorous quantification via Louis (1982) Observed Fisher Information, Profile Likelihood, and Nonparametric/Parametric Bootstrap (B = 400) demonstrates that π̂g is statistically weakly identified, has wide 95% confidence intervals spanning 30–90 percentage points of [0.05, 0.95], and lacks statistical power:

  • Large Per-Variant Sampling Noise & Small Effective Sample Size (Kish ESS): At AF ≤ 0.001, ~60% of coding variants are singletons/doubletons with median log-OR standard error median(SEgv) = 1.633. Consequently, even genes with Mg = 200–500 rare coding variants have an inverse-variance Kish Effective Sample Size ESS = (Σ wv)2 / Σ wv2 of only 10 to 57 effective variants (e.g., CSF2RB ESS = 13.6; CTSA ESS = 16.9; AGER ESS = 10.1).
  • Missing-Data Fisher Information Collapse (Louis 1982): For the vast majority of rare variants with low-to-moderate moderator scores, the risk and protective branches overlap within 1×SEgv, yielding ambiguous posterior responsibilities τgv ≈ π̂g (up to 86%–97% of variants in NOD2, PIEZO1, CARD9, and SYNE1 fall in τgv ∈ [0.25, 0.75]). By Louis's identity, Iobs(πg) = Icomp(πg) − Imiss(πg) = ESS · Σ w̃v (τgv − π̂g)2 / [π̂g2(1 − π̂g)2], so missing-data uncertainty cancels nearly all complete-data information.
  • Parameter-Free Folded-Normal Magnitude Dispersion (M9) Achieves High Power Without πg: Whereas the 2-component vs. 1-component EM Likelihood Ratio Test (LRT) fails to reach exome-wide significance on any gene (pLRT ≥ 0.0025), the 3-d.f. Folded-Normal Magnitude Dispersion WLS test on |β̂gv − β̄w,g| estimates zero mixture proportions and achieves pmag = 8.01×10−12 on CTSA, 1.42×10−10 on CSF2RB, and 6.26×10−12 on ZNF341.
