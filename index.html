4D. Incorporating Risk & Protective Effects in Rare-Variant Meta-Regression: Model 2 (M8: 4-d.f. Joint Location + Slope) & Model 3 (M9: 3-d.f. Folded-Normal Magnitude Dispersion & Bidirectional Omnibus)

Standard 3-d.f. functional slope meta-regression (M7) tests only whether variant log-odds ratios covary monotonically with functional severity (H0: γAM = γLoF = γCon = 0) after centering out the gene-level intercept β0g. Consequently, M7 can miss two critical classes of disease genes: (1) Uniform Net Protective or Risk Coding Shifts (e.g., ACVR2A, SENP8, DNMT3A, TNFRSF6B), where damaging coding variants shift the entire gene baseline relative to neutral synonymous variants (Δβ0g ≠ 0, Zloc,g ≠ 0); and (2) Within-Gene Bidirectional Allelic Series (e.g., CSF2RB, CTSA, ZNF341, LPIN1, HECTD1), where some damaging variants confer risk (+βgv > 0) while others confer protection (−βgv < 0), causing signed slopes to cancel out.

Model 2 (M8): Joint 4-d.f. Location + Slope Omnibus Meta-Regression

Calibrates each gene's non-synonymous inverse-variance mean against the cohort × Firth-fallback synonymous neutral baseline (μ̂0,syn) to form an orthogonal 1-d.f. net coding location shift Zloc,g (< 0 for net protective coding burden; > 0 for net risk coding burden), unified with the 3-d.f. within-gene moderator slope test F3,Mg−4M7:

F4, Mg−4M8 = ( Zloc,g² + 3 · F3, Mg−4M7 ) / 4 ∼ F(4, Mg − 4)

💊 How Human Genetic Direction of Effect Dictates Therapeutic Modality in Inflammatory Bowel Disease

Two canonical protective coding variants in IBD established the gold standard for genetics-guided immunology drug discovery: (1) IL23R p.Arg381Gln (rs11209026, common AF ≈ 6.0%, OR ≈ 0.40), a loss-of-function (LoF) variant in the cytoplasmic JAK2/STAT3-docking tail of the IL-23 receptor that directly catalyzed selective anti-IL-23p19 monoclonal antibodies (Risankizumab, Mirikizumab, Guselkumab), oral IL-23R macrocyclic peptides (Icotrokinra / JNJ-2113), and allosteric TYK2 inhibitors (Deucravacitinib); and (2) CARD9 c.1434+1G>C (rs141992399, low-frequency AF ≈ 0.45%, OR ≈ 0.39), a C-terminal splice-site truncation that abolishes TRIM62-mediated K27 ubiquitination and dampens Dectin-1/SYK → CARD9–BCL10–MALT1 (CBM) NF-κB signaling in myeloid cells.
